## Paper transcription checks

Primary source: the official CBSE 2015 Class X paper, Outside Delhi, code 30/1. It states there are 31 questions, with 4 in Section A, 6 in Section B, 10 in Section C, and 11 in Section D. The Class X solution source contains Q1--Q31 and follows those exact section boundaries. The independent Outside Delhi 30/1 marking scheme was also consulted; its expected numeric results agree with the checked answers below. See [the official CBSE paper/marking-scheme index](https://www.cbse.gov.in/curric~1/qpms2015/qpms2015.htm) and a searchable [30/1 marking-scheme copy](https://files.supercop.in/cbse-board-papers/class10/maths/ans_m_2015.pdf).

- Q2: official text is `20 sqrt(3) m` for the shadow, not `203 m` as it appears in the public LaTeX transcription. The official value yields `tan(theta)=1/sqrt(3)`, hence `theta=30 degrees`.
- Q4: figure placement has `R` on the minor arc, so the intercepted angle `PRQ` is obtuse and equals `120 degrees`.
- Q6: tangent lengths from the same point and `Area = inradius * semiperimeter` give `AB=9 cm`, `AC=12 cm`.
- Q8: the two given AP sums yield the matrix system `[12 31; 10 45][a;d]=[167;235]`, with solution `(a,d)=(1,5)`.
- Q9: `p=7` collapses `B` and `C`; discard it as degenerate, leaving `p=4`.
- Q13: constant altitude means the horizontal range changes from 1500 m to 4500 m; speed is 720 km/h.
- Q17: canvas area excludes the open floor/base; 100-tent total is Rs 759,000 and the stated 50% contribution is Rs 379,500.
- Q19: exposed area excludes the circular cube/hemisphere interface; area is 678.5 cm^2 and painting cost is Rs 33.925 (about Rs 33.93).
- Q29: determinant is `2k^2 + 3k + 21`; setting twice the area to 48 gives `2k^2 + 3k - 27=0`, so `k=3` or `k=-9/2`.
- Q30: each shaded cap is a 90-degree segment of a circle with `r^2=882`; total shaded area is 504 m^2.
- Q31: removing two hemispheres removes one sphere's volume; a wire of diameter 1.4 cm has radius 0.7 cm, producing length 158.4 cm.
